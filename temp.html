About

I am a bioinformatician at SR-Tiget (San Raffaele Telethon Institute for Gene Therapy) in Milan, focused on developing data analysis pipelines and tools for scientific research. My work sits at the intersection of computational biology, software engineering, and data science.

My primary languages are Python and R. I work extensively with single-cell omics data, bulk RNAseq, and custom bioinformatics pipelines. Alongside wet-lab-adjacent computational work I enjoy building reusable packages and automation tools.
